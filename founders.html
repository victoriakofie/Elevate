The founders

Two friends who started with six school fees.

Elevate began in 2019 when its founders paid school fees for six children out of their own pockets. Today that idea runs across 27 communities.

Co-Founder & Executive Director

Ross Nimo-Tieku

Ross grew up in the same communities Elevate now serves. After a decade in education policy, he came back home to build the organization he wished had existed for his own classmates.

"We're not handing out charity. We're handing back a starting line."

Co-Founder & Director of Programs

Victoria Kofie

Victoria spent eight years designing vocational training programs before co-founding Elevate. She now leads the employer partnerships that turn graduates into hires.

"A diploma matters most on the day it becomes a paycheck."

Back the founders' mission.

Every donation goes directly toward the classrooms and job placements Ross and Victoria set out to build.
