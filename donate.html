What your donation covers

Every gift is split between immediate household support and long-term education or job placement.

  • ₵50Feeds a family of four for a week.
  • ₵200Covers one term of school fees and supplies.
  • ₵500Funds a month of vocational training for a young adult.
  • ₵1,500Sponsors a full job-placement track through to a first paycheck.

Elevate is a registered nonprofit. Donations may be tax-deductible depending on your country of residence.
