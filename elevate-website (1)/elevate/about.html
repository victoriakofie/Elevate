About Elevate

A direct line between generosity and opportunity.

Elevate was founded on a simple belief: poverty isn't a lack of ability, it's a lack of access. We close that gap with schooling and real jobs.

The problem

Millions of capable young people are stuck at the starting line.

Across the communities we work in, families spend most of a day securing food and water, leaving little room for school. Without an education, young adults struggle to find stable work — and the cycle repeats for the next generation.

We saw the same pattern in every village we visited: enormous potential, and almost no access to the two things that break the cycle — classrooms and jobs.

Our approach

Education first, then a real job.

Donations first cover the basics that keep a child in class — fees, uniforms, meals. From there, teenagers and young adults move into our vocational tracks: trades, digital skills and small-business training, paired with local employer partners who commit to hiring graduates.

We measure success the same way a family would: is the next paycheck theirs?
